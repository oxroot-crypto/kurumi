# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Kurumi is committed to a welcoming, harassment-free environment for everyone,
regardless of experience, identity, background, or role in the project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give and accept technical feedback without personal attacks.
- Keep discussions focused on the code, the data, and the problem.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or sustained disruption.
- Insults, personal attacks, or derogatory comments.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
and other project spaces.

## Reporting

Do not open a public issue containing personal or sensitive details. Open a
minimal issue asking the maintainers for a private channel, or contact the
repository owner through GitHub at <https://github.com/oxroot-crypto>. If the
concern involves a maintainer, use GitHub's report abuse flow at
<https://github.com/contact/report-abuse>.

Maintainers will review reports in good faith, keep them confidential where
possible, and take proportionate action.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions, reject
contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Decisions
will be explained to the people involved when it is safe to do so.
